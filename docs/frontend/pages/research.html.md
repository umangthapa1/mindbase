# research.html

Research and intelligence gathering page for the Mindbase AI application.

## Purpose

This page provides an interface for conducting research and gathering information using AI assistance, including:
- Initiating research queries on various topics
- Viewing research results and summaries
- Saving research findings to memory
- Organizing research with tags and categories
- Exporting research reports
- Iterative research refinement

## Contents

- Research query input with natural language support
- Research results display with source citations
- AI-generated summaries and key points
- Related topics and follow-up questions
- Research saving options (to memory, documents, etc.)
- Research history and session management
- Collaboration features for shared research (if available)

## Usage

Access this page from the main navigation. It interacts with the backend research service to perform queries using AI models and presents results in an organized, readable format. Research can be saved to the memory system for future reference.
